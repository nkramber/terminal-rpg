# The cast

Status: world file, v1. Owner: Nate. Started 2026-09-13 in the cast block of the world-building interview (D-146, D-262). Written in ASD-STE100 (D-10).

This file holds the story characters: who they are, what they do best, and why they stay with the lead. Its source is the decision register, entries D-267 onward. Each fact cites its decision. The order in which the lead meets each character belongs to the arc.

The text that the player reads has its own voice (D-11). This file gives facts for writers, not player text.

## How a character works

No character has a job or a class (D-268, D-272). A character gains abilities from lessons, and any character can equip any lesson (D-274, D-278).

- A lesson is a rite or a drill (D-275). A rite teaches a spell, on a page or a lead strip. A drill teaches a physical skill as a written form.
- A lesson holds no power. Every caster draws on the thing below (D-148).
- A rite with a church stamp is legal. A rite with no stamp can hang its user (D-130, D-275).

Each character has two aptitudes (D-274):

- The main aptitude is the kind of ability that the character does best.
- The side aptitude is a second kind. A personal task in the hubs unlocks it, and the menu hides it until then (D-282, D-283). A player can miss the task.

The eight kinds (D-281):

| Kind | Lesson | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Mend | Rite | Healing |
| Harm | Rite | Damage spells |
| Blight | Rite | Poison, sleep, and other statuses |
| Boon | Rite | Shields and haste |
| Blade | Drill | Melee |
| Guard | Drill | Protect allies |
| Shot | Drill | Crossbow and bow |
| Theft | Drill | Steal, locks, and traps |

The rules of the aptitudes:

- No two characters share a main aptitude, except the replacement of D-279, which can share the main aptitude of the dead character (D-274).
- No two characters in the whole game share a side aptitude (D-271, D-274).
- A side aptitude never matches the main aptitude of the same character (D-281).
- The whole cast holds eight characters at most, the replacement included, because each needs a side aptitude of its own (D-281).

How lessons sit, grow, and reach the party is open (OQ-38 to OQ-47).

## The lead

One character is the lead (D-267). Scenes center on the lead, and the dialogue choices of the player are the lines of the lead. The lead can wait in reserve like any other character, but the map always follows the lead (D-292).

## The party of region one

Five characters join during region one: four local and one from the enemy crown (D-280). The party grows as the lead meets each one (D-268). Each has a reason of their own that lies on the road of the lead, and the party has no single cause (D-287).

| Name | Who | Origin | Age | Main aptitude | Side aptitude |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marrek, a man | The lead, a commoner of the mining town | Local | 19 | Blade | Guard |
| Bergit, a woman | A warden of the miners' guild, who lost her mark | Local | 31 | Guard | Shot |
| Dagvar, a man | A hexer of the old faith | Local | 35 | Harm | Blight |
| Ottild, a woman | A cutpurse and smuggler | Local | 21 | Theft | Blade |
| Elio, a man | A clerk of the license office of the foreign church | Enemy crown | 24 | Mend | Boon |

The names, genders, and ages come from D-297 and D-298. The aptitudes come from D-285, D-289, and D-293.

Every character is an adult (D-298). The session reads the limit of D-126 as a rule for the whole party, because every character takes wounds in battle.

The four test sprites of D-233 become Bergit, Dagvar, Elio, and Ottild (D-289). Marrek needs a new sprite.

### Marrek, the lead

Marrek grew up in the mining town under the ban (D-284). He fights up close with what a miner carries (D-285). A parent of Marrek went down into the deep mine and did not come back, and he goes down to learn why (D-291). His road starts at the thing below.

### Bergit, the warden

The guild took the mark of Bergit because she refused an order in the mines (D-294). She knows something the guild hides, and the search of Marrek needs it. She has no standing left with the guild, so the guild reaches the party through a grudge.

### Dagvar, the hexer

Dagvar casts with no church license, so the law can hang him (D-130). He keeps the old-faith rites at the waystones that the church breaks, and the road of Marrek passes the stones (D-295). He was an adult when the ban came, so he remembers the faith before it (D-298).

### Ottild, the cutpurse

Ottild smuggles goods through old mine tunnels and knows ways into the deep mine (D-296). Marrek needs the way, and she needs his blade. That reason ends when Marrek reaches the bottom, so a second reason must grow.

### Elio, the clerk

Elio comes from the land of the enemy crown and stamps rites at the license office of the foreign church (D-288). The church trained him in mend (D-289). The church or the governor sends him to travel with the party and report (D-290). He stays as a watcher who can turn, and the relationship with Elio decides what he reports.

A legal healer and an illegal caster share the party (D-289). Elio holds the only main mend in region one.

## Later characters

Three characters join after region one: two new characters and the replacement (D-299).

- The main aptitude of each new character is boon, blight, or shot (D-281, D-299).
- The side aptitudes of the three are harm, mend, and theft (D-293).
- Later characters can come from anywhere, the old crown included (D-273).

## The death

The story kills one character in the whole game, at a set point, and the player has no choice in it (D-270, D-279). The replacement can share the main aptitude of the dead character, and its side aptitude differs (D-274). Who dies, and where, belong to the arc block.

## Names

The names follow the two sound palettes of `setting.md` (D-159). The four local names sound hard and closed, and the foreign name Elio sounds soft and open. Elio can take three syllables, because rule 11 of the `game-text-style` skill allows three for a foreign name (D-300).
